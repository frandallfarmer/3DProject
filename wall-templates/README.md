This project was inspired by the paper terrain from Paper Realms.

See https://paperrealms.com for links to their various paper terrain models, including several different wall designs (some shown in the photos you can see in the /media directory.)

These .png files are templates you can use to create your own custom textured walls: Just load them into your favorite image editor and paste your wall texture between the silver posts, print, and cut.

The ./cutfiles subdirectory contains files for use with Silhouette cutting machines.
